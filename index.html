HOW TO USE
  1. Open the Arena in your game and go to the Storm Arena tab.
  2. Screen record while slowly scrolling through the full player list — go all the way to the bottom.
  3. Upload the recording below. The tool will extract every player it can find.
  4. When done, go to Share / Import and export a JSON. Send it to your war planner.
  5. Your war planner imports all JSONs → sees the full bracket in War Dashboard.
⚡ Processing happens entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Works on Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge.
